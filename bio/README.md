# A Little Longer

<img src="portrait.png" alt="A portrait by the water" width="600">

Gentle, quietly determined, and fond of sunny days. Perhaps that gives you a rough sketch of who I am.

*A Little Longer* brings together moments I’ve wanted to linger over: sunlight on the grass, people by the sea, a courtyard as evening falls... These photographs hold a little of what it felt like to be there.
